❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my Frydgi Pro subscription?

On iPhone/iPad: Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions → Frydgi and tap Cancel Subscription. On Android: Go to Google Play → Subscriptions → Frydgi and tap Cancel.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Frydgi → go to the Profile tab → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes all your data. Alternatively, email us and we'll delete it for you within 48 hours.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

The app is crashing or not working properly.

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall Frydgi. If you're still having trouble, email us with your device model and a description of what's happening.

Why isn't the AI generating recipes?

Make sure you have ingredients added to your pantry and an active internet connection. Free-tier users have a weekly recipe limit — upgrading to Pro gives unlimited generation.

Can I use Frydgi on multiple devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on up to 3 devices. Your pantry, recipes, and meal plans sync automatically.

I was charged but didn't receive Pro access.

Go to Profile → tap "Restore Purchases". If the issue persists, email us with your order confirmation and we'll resolve it promptly.
